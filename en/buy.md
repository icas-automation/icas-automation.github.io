# Ordering a License

All **ICAS Automation** modules are licensed and assigned to a unique **license key**, linked directly to the **Serial Number** of your Fibaro Home Center 3 or Yubii Home hub.

Currently, we handle the sales process directly, allowing us to provide full technical support at every stage of the process.

## Step-by-Step Purchase Process

### Step 1: Prepare your order message using the template below

**Email Subject:**
> ICAS Module Order – `[Enter module name]`

**Email Body:**
> * **Selected module:** `[Module name]` *e.g., Surplus Energy Switch*
> * **Hub serial number:** `[Serial number]` *e.g., HC3-00001234*
> * **Additional notes or questions:** `[Notes]` *e.g., Example note or question*

### Step 2: Send your order
Send the prepared order message to **icas.automation@gmail.com**.

### Step 3: Receive a confirmation email
You will receive an email with a confirmation and payment details.

### Step 4: Module delivery
Once the payment is credited, we will send a personalized, individual license key directly to your email address, along with activation instructions.

::: danger Delivery Time
The key will be delivered within **1–2 business days**.
:::

## How to find your HC3 Serial Number?

The serial number is required to generate the license key. You can find it in two places:

1. **In the Fibaro panel:** Log in to your Fibaro HC3 hub and go to **Settings**, then to **General**. You will find the serial number in the **Hub Information** section. The number format is e.g., `HC3-00001234`.
2. **On the casing:** The number is located on the rating plate on the bottom of the device.

## Important Information

* **Perpetual License:** You are purchasing a lifetime license assigned to your specific HC3 hub.
* **Unlimited Instances:** A single license assigned to your HC3 hub entitles you to run an unlimited number of QuickApp instances. You can control any number of devices in your home without additional fees.
* **Access to Updates:** Using your purchased license key, you can update the software at any time. You can easily download the latest versions of the modules from our official website and install them yourself, without needing to contact us.
* **Processing Time:** We typically deliver the license key within **1–2 business days** of payment confirmation.
