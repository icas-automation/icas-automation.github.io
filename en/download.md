# Downloads

Welcome to the central repository for ICAS Automation modules. Here you can download the latest stable `.fqax` (Fibaro QuickApp) files for your Home Center 3 / Yubii Home hub.

::: info Important
Always remember to back up your HC3 hub configuration before installing new modules or performing updates.
:::

## Surplus Energy Switch

A module for intelligent management of solar energy surpluses. It autonomously redirects excess power to specified devices, such as water heaters, thermal buffers, or air conditioning.

::: info `v.1.0`
* **Release Date**: 2026.07.26
* **Description**: Initial commercial release
:::
