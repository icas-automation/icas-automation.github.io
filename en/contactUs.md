# About Us

**ICAS Automation** was born from an engineering passion for excellence and a drive to create building automation that operates reliably, discreetly, and 100% locally.

We don't believe in half-measures or complex, cluttered interfaces that demand constant user attention. We create advanced software extensions (QuickApps) for **Fibaro Home Center 3** and **Nice** systems, granting them full decision-making autonomy.

## Our Values

* **Engineering Precision:** Every QuickApp module undergoes multi-stage testing in real-world Smart Home environments.
* **100% Cloud-Free:** Your data and household habits stay inside your home. Our algorithms execute entirely inside your hub's processor.
* **Maximum Usability:** We build intuitive, easy-to-use software that fully unleashes the power and advanced capabilities of Fibaro systems.
