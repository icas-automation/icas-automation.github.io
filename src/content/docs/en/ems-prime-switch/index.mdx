## Is your smart home truly optimizing energy costs, or just passively watching them?

If you own a photovoltaic system integrated with a Fibaro HC3 gateway, you are likely feeding valuable energy back into the grid for pennies every single day. Even worse—unthoughtful automations that switch on your devices, such as water heaters or heat pumps, during passing clouds can damage their expensive electronics due to relay chattering.

**ICAS EMS Prime Switch changes the game**. It is an advanced QuickApp that empowers the Fibaro ecosystem with missing decision-making algorithms, turning passive monitoring into a maintenance-free, cascading cost-saving engine.

Installation is as simple as downloading the file, importing it into your Fibaro HC3, and assigning the ID of the controlled device. Zero LUA scripts, zero frustration—the app instantly guides you through a straightforward setup and calibration.
Once you open the panel in your Fibaro system, you get a clean control hub that gives you complete command over your energy management:

Track real energy flows across your entire installation live. In real time, the system analyzes solar production, current home consumption, and the precise level of energy surplus or grid import.

### Complete overview of status and operational parameters

Monitor the current state of your managed device along with its operating mode all in one place. Additionally, continuously preview key decision-making rules: calculated required power, configured surplus coverage thresholds, stabilized delay timers, and minimum runtime settings.

### Energy surplus management panel

See at a glance which turn-on conditions are currently met, the remaining time before launch after surplus stabilization, and the guaranteed operating cycle duration that protects your equipment from wear. ### Precise self-consumption analytics and real savings

The application automatically measures free solar kilowatt-hours retained in your home and converts them in real time into estimated financial savings. Energy statistics are neatly categorized and reset across daily, weekly, monthly, and annual cycles, allowing you to see the exact money kept in your pocket at any time.

### Flexible operating mode control

The default mode is full automation (Auto) powered by EMS Prime Switch’s intelligent algorithms. However, at any moment, you can force manual operation (Run) or completely lock out the device (Lock) while away on vacation or during routine maintenance.
