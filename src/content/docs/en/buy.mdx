## Step-by-Step Purchase Process

<Steps>

1. Prepare your order details according to the template below:

   :::note[Order Template]
   **Order – [Enter module name]**
   ```text
   Selected module: [Module name]
   Serial number: [Serial number]
   Additional notes or questions: [Notes]
   ```
    Example:

   **Order – EMS Prime Switch**
   ```text
   Selected module: EMS Prime Switch
   Serial number: HC3-00001234
   Additional notes or questions: Example note or question
   ```

2. Send your prepared order to icas.automation@gmail.com.

3. You will receive a confirmation email with payment details.

4. Once the payment is cleared, we will activate your license, and the system will automatically fetch its updated status to your hub.
</Steps>

## How to Find Your HC3 Serial Number?
The serial number is required to place an order. You can find it in two places:

<Steps>
1. In the Fibaro Panel: Log in to your Fibaro HC3 gateway and go to Settings, then General. You will find the serial number in the Gateway Information section. The format looks like: HC3-00001234.
2. On the Device Casing: The number is printed on the rating plate on the bottom of the device.
</Steps>

3. Access to Updates: Using your purchased license, you can update the software at any time. You can seamlessly download the latest module versions from our official website and install them yourself, without needing to contact us.
