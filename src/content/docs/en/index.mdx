ICAS Automation is a fresh initiative in the Smart Home ecosystem, born from years of hands-on experience with home automation systems. After using these setups daily for years, we know exactly what Fibaro lacks from an end-user perspective to become a truly complete ecosystem. We build advanced, integrated components and fully autonomous extensions (QuickApps) for the HC3 controller to bridge those gaps. Our name is our technology pledge.
